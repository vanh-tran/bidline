# Bidline

Small US government contractors lose days reading 400-page tenders, only to find a hard stop that rules them out (a minimum dock depth, a certification they don't hold), or to get rejected over a detail they missed.

Bidline reads a SAM.gov tender for them. It finds every deadline, hard stop and trap with its page number, builds the compliance matrix and answers one question: can we bid on this? Built for 1–50 person firms, not for proposal departments.

**Status:** R0 in progress. Eval scores, latency and cost will be posted here from real runs.
